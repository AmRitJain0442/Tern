# Community conduct

Tern welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Be respectful, explain technical disagreements with evidence, and help others participate. Critique the work without attacking the person.

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, unwanted sexual attention, spam, and disclosure of another person's private information are not acceptable in project issues, pull requests or other project spaces.

The maintainer, [AmRitJain0442](https://github.com/AmRitJain0442), may edit or remove harmful content, warn participants, or restrict participation. Enforcement should be proportionate to the conduct and its impact.

To request private help, open an issue titled **Private moderation contact requested** with no incident details or personal information, or use an existing private channel to the maintainer. The maintainer will arrange a private follow-up. Do not post another person's identity or sensitive evidence publicly. For software vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
